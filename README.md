# Restaurant User Data - Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

This project performs a comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis on a dataset of 200 restaurant customers. The goal is to uncover demographic trends, dining preferences, and factors influencing customer satisfaction to provide actionable business insights.

## 📊 Project Overview
The analysis investigates how variables like age, gender, marital status, and location impact restaurant ratings and spending habits. By understanding these patterns, restaurants can better tailor their marketing and service strategies.

## 🗂️ Dataset Description
The dataset contains 15 columns, including:
* **Demographics:** Gender, Year of Birth (YOB), Marital Status, Activity (Student/Professional), and Location.
* **Preferences:** Cuisines (Japanese, French, Indian, etc.), Alcohol consumption, and Smoking habits.
* **Ratings:** Food Rating, Service Rating, and Overall Rating (on a scale of 1-5).
* **Behavior:** Budget and Dining frequency (Alone vs. Socially).

## 🚀 Key Insights
* **Popular Cuisines:** Japanese and French cuisines are the most preferred, making up over 70% of the entries.
* **Demographic Trends:** The user base is primarily composed of young adults (25-34) and a secondary group of seniors (50+).
* **Rating Correlation:** Food and Service ratings are largely independent, but both strongly contribute to the **Overall Rating**.
* **Spending Habits:** The highest average budgets are found in the 10-19 and 40-49 age demographics.
* **Social Habits:** Across all age groups, there is a high frequency of dining alone or non-socially.
